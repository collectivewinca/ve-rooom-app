# Governance

VE Rooom is an open-source project stewarded by **VE Lab**.

## Principles

- Decisions are made in the open whenever security or privacy does not require confidentiality.
- Maintainers optimize for user safety, deployability, and long-term maintenance.
- Contributors are evaluated on the quality of their work and collaboration, not affiliation.
- Backward compatibility is preferred, but APIs may change before version `1.0.0`.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who submits code, documentation, testing, design, translations, or useful issue reports under the project's contribution terms.

### Reviewers

Trusted contributors who regularly review work in an area of the project. Reviewers may label and triage issues but do not merge without maintainer authorization.

### Maintainers

Maintainers can merge pull requests, publish releases, moderate community spaces, and make security decisions. The initial maintainer is the `collectivewinca` GitHub account on behalf of VE Lab.

New maintainers are nominated by an existing maintainer based on sustained, constructive contributions and demonstrated judgment. Maintainer status may be removed for inactivity, repeated policy violations, or project risk, following a documented maintainer decision.

## Decision process

- Routine changes: one maintainer approval and passing required checks.
- Significant architecture, dependency, license, pricing, or data-retention changes: public proposal in an issue or Discussion, a reasonable comment period, and explicit maintainer approval.
- Security incidents: maintainers may act privately and immediately, then publish an appropriate advisory after remediation.
- If consensus is not reached, the lead maintainer makes the decision and documents the rationale.

## Releases

VE Rooom follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Releases are tagged, described in GitHub Releases, and recorded in [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).

## Project identity

The Apache-2.0 license covers the source code. The VE Rooom name and VE Lab branding identify this project and do not grant permission to imply endorsement or official status for a modified deployment.
