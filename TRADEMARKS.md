The Apache-2.0 license grants rights to use, modify, and distribute the source code. It does not grant rights to use the VE Rooom or VE Lab names, logos, or branding in a way that suggests endorsement or official status.

You may accurately state that a product is "based on VE Rooom" or "powered by VE Rooom" and link to this repository. Modified public deployments should use a distinct name and clearly identify the party operating them.

Unmodified community deployments may retain the VE Rooom name when they clearly state that they are independently operated and provide their own privacy/support contact.

Questions about branding can be directed to the maintainers listed in [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).
